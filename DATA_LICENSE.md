The root [MIT License](LICENSE) covers the software and its usage documentation. It does not relicense MEV annotations, example data, paper figures, or third-party video content.

The authoritative dataset terms are the [MEV Dataset License on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MultiEventVideo/MEV/blob/main/LICENSE).

- **MEV-created annotations and metadata** (including labels, timestamps, prompts, splits, source manifests, and evaluation metadata) are released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), unless a file states otherwise. This includes the MEV annotation and prompt excerpts in `mev_eval/examples/`. Give appropriate credit, link the license, and indicate changes.
- **Video assets** remain subject to their source-specific licenses and platform terms. The official video package is directly downloadable; users should follow the applicable source terms and preserve available attribution and provenance. Optional restricted or premium source versions are not automatically part of that package.
- **Paper figures in `assets/`** are reproduced from the MEV paper to illustrate the dataset. Embedded video frames retain the rights and terms of their original sources; the code license grants no additional rights to those frames. See [asset provenance](assets/README.md).

For dataset questions or to initiate a provenance/correction request, use the [dataset discussion page](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MultiEventVideo/MEV/discussions). Refer to the official dataset license for its takedown and update policy.
